The Manifesto

WE ARE NOT A HEALTH CLUB. WE ARE A STRENGTH LABORATORY.

We do not trade in vanity, mirrors, or screen-heavy distractions. KRAFTHAUS was founded in Hamburg to reclaim the physical truth of conditioning: consistent progression under bar, raw effort, and precise anatomical form.

  1. 1

    Coached Every Session

    You never lift alone. Every class is fully programmed and closely supervised by active practitioners.

  2. 2

    Metric-Driven Progress

    We track structural outputs, not calories. Your logs dictate your target weight assemblies.

  3. 3

    No Mirror Ego

    No vanity walls. Our focus is entirely centered on functional mechanics and collective drive.

Built by coaches, not a franchise template.

Training Programs

Built for Raw Power and Longevity

Our training is segmented into focused physical disciplines. Run on a rolling seasonal cycle.

  • Kraft / Strength

    Pure barbell mechanics, heavy compounds, and progressive overload. Structured around powerlifts and raw athletic resistance to build skeletal density.

    • Barbell
    • Powerlifting
    • Hypertrophy
    Booking

  • Conditioning / HIT

    High intensity operational engines. Incorporates sleds, rowers, assault bikes and kettlebells to build an explosive aerobic and anaerobic threshold.

    • Kettlebell
    • Sled Pushes
    • Anaerobic
    Booking

  • Mobility / Resilience

    Structural joint integrity, active stretching, and restorative postures. Designed to bulletproof connective tissues and accelerate structural recovery.

    • Anatomy
    • Joint Mobility
    • Recovery
